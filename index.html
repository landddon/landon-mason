About me

A trusted advisor to customers, from first conversation to renewal and expansion.

I’m a B2B SaaS professional who owns the full commercial lifecycle: prospecting and closing new business, then managing the account after the sale to drive renewal, expansion, and long-term success.

My approach is consultative. I learn what a customer is trying to achieve, build an account plan around it, and use usage data and buyer signals to spot growth opportunities and head off churn before it happens. I present business cases to technical and executive stakeholders and work closely with Sales Engineering and Product to deliver for the customer.

I’m based in Seattle, WA, and a graduate of Western Washington University (B.S. Business & Marketing).
